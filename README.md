# Da Pitch ⚽⏱️

**Da Pitch** is a lightning-fast, minimalist Progressive Web App (PWA) designed specifically for football coaches along the sideline. Unlike bloated team management apps, Da Pitch focuses purely on what matters during a live match: real-time stopwatch tracking, fair playing time distribution, and seamless rotation management.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Live Sideline Stopwatch:** A real-time running timer (with pause and match end states) that tracks exact playing minutes per player second-by-second.
* **Visual Playing Time Analytics:** Instant color-coded indicators and progress bars showing each player's relative playing time compared to the total match duration (<25%, <50%, <75%, >75%).
* **Quick Line-Up & Substitution Blocks:** Easily map out minute blocks (e.g., minute 0, 15, 27) and instantly drag or tap players between the field and the bench.
* **Tactical Formats:** Switch effortlessly between formations across different match formats (11v11, 8v8, and 6v6).
* **Goal & Assist Tracker:** Record goals and assists tied directly to players on the field, complete with a live score evolution timeline.
* **Universal Share:** Generate and copy a clean, professional match summary or playing time overview to share directly via WhatsApp with your team.
* **PWA & Offline Ready:** Install it directly to your iPhone or Android home screen via Safari/Chrome shortcuts. Your local lineup and player data remain securely saved on your device across updates.

## 📱 Designed for Coaches
Da Pitch gives you absolute control over the dugout without the administrative clutter—keeping rotations smooth and playing time fair for everyone on the squad.
